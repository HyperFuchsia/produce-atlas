The Framework · The Integrity System

The Whole Male Integrity Architecture

Integrity is an interacting architecture, not a single moral label. Pressure in one domain can compromise several others — and the final visible failure is real, but it is not the whole causal system.

The starting observationWhole-person well-being

Male well-being is not reducible to muscularity, sexual performance, income, occupational status, visible composure, relationship retention, or the ability to continue functioning while deteriorating internally. A role is not healthy merely because it is still being performed.

A man may:

  • continue working while sleep, pain, stress, or illness erodes biological integrity;
  • preserve social status while losing psychological stability;
  • preserve a relationship while abandoning boundaries and self-trust;
  • preserve a masculine appearance while losing connection to health, conscience, identity, or meaningful choice;
  • or appear responsible while being denied the information, authority, recovery, and feedback needed to act responsibly.

The project’s moral objection is not to work, provision, protection, sacrifice, partnership, or leadership. It is to allowing a role to survive by consuming the person inside it.

Core standard

The role must not survive by sacrificing the male person inside it.

The taxonomyFrom thirty-five dimensions to one architecture

The permanent framework identified thirty-five named dimensions of integrity, each defined by what it protects. That breadth is valuable — but it risks making “integrity” a synonym for everything good, and it risks implying thirty-five coequal scores. The more defensible architecture, adopted in the July 2026 revision, groups internal capacities, ethical dimensions, relational participation, and environmental determinants rather than presenting a flat list.

Institutional clarification

Nothing has been dropped. Every one of the original thirty-five dimensions appears below with its full definition, organized into seven groups. The revision also names domains the flat list under-emphasized — sleep and circadian regulation; pain, illness, injury, and recovery; trauma and stress; consent; practical agency; supported decision-making; intimate and family life; cultural participation; power integrity; and the expanded existential and surrounding-conditions groups. Where the two documents differ, the grouped architecture governs. See the correction record.

The architecture as a whole protects male integrity itself: the preservation of the whole male person against reduction to function, status, anatomy, labor, or sacrifice. No single group below contains the person; together they describe him.

Group sixExistential integrity

Recognition that the man’s life has value beyond performance, function, status, health, and social approval. The July 2026 architecture expands this from a single line into a group with five strands:

  • meaning and purpose;
  • hope and future orientation;
  • creativity, play, rest, and curiosity;
  • mortality, legacy, and intergenerational concern;
  • and freedom of religious, spiritual, philosophical, or atheistic commitment.

None of these is a luxury appended to a productive life. They are part of what the architecture exists to protect — the reasons a life is worth sustaining, not merely the machinery that sustains it.

Group sevenThe conditions surrounding integrity

These are not internal virtues, and they must not be scored as though they belonged only to the man. They are the environmental determinants within which every internal domain above either holds or erodes — the subject of the environment and reciprocity chapter.

The point of itWhy the taxonomy matters: causal cascades

The architecture is designed to make causal cascades visible. Occupational extraction can damage sleep and physiological regulation. Degraded sleep can weaken attention, inhibition, working memory, and decisional stability. Poor decisions can destabilize relationships and work. Shame may then convert a temporary or treatable difficulty into a total judgment of failed character or failed manhood — what the framework calls a masculine failure code. The final visible failure is real, but it is not the whole causal system.

The final visible symptom must therefore never be isolated from the chain that preceded it. The purpose of the domains is to identify support, risk, responsibility, and intervention — not to rank human worth. Seeing the chain does not erase individual responsibility; it locates responsibility accurately, including the responsibility of the institutions that built the conditions. How to weigh those competing responsibilities is the subject of moral proportionality.

The prohibitionNever a “whole-man score”

Limit

The taxonomy must never become a “whole-man score.” The site’s governance standard prohibits dignity, masculinity, character, and wellness scoring. The same rule governs the site’s tools: the well-being check-in reflects your answers back to you; it does not compute a score, a rank, or a diagnosis.

A man is not a dashboard. The architecture describes where support, risk, and responsibility live so that each can be addressed where it actually is — and so that no single domain, least of all a socially visible one, is mistaken for the whole person. Dignity itself sits outside every table on this page: it is inherent and non-forfeitable, as the next chapter establishes.

Dignity establishes the value of the man. Biological integrity protects the living person. Ethical and moral integrity direct agency. Character integrity makes those commitments reliable. Sovereign integrity protects authorship of the life.
