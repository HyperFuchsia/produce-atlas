About this film

Fifteen minutes, played straight through, with score and sound. Nothing here is a file: every figure is drawn from line segments and every sound is built from oscillators and filtered noise, all generated while it plays. That is why the whole film is one portable page.

The score is written in D minor and follows the documentary's own architecture: a bass, a sustained string or choir pad, and a melodic line moving through a real chord progression. A felt-piano motif stays unresolved for fifteen minutes and lands on its major third only in the final bar. A pulse enters at the biology sequence and slows as the day wears down. The load transfer is a string cluster that adds a voice and drifts further out of tune with each pass.

Everything plays into a generated convolution reverb, and each scene sets its own send — a long tail in the cave, almost none on the open plain, a dead room in the office — so the space changes when the place does. Three passages are scored as silence on purpose; the audio drops out completely and comes back.
