About this film

Fifteen minutes, played straight through, with score and sound. Nothing here is a file: every figure is drawn from line segments and every sound is built from oscillators and filtered noise, all generated while it plays. That is why the whole film is one portable page.

The score follows the documentary's own architecture — a felt piano motif that stays unresolved for fifteen minutes, a pulse that enters at the biology sequence and slows as the day wears down, a microtonal string cluster over the load transfer, and a final chord that resolves only on the last note. Three passages are scored as silence on purpose; the audio drops out completely and comes back.
