### Thesis

Human civilization cannot sustainably flourish if the dignity, integrity, biological health, ethical development, environmental conditions, and sovereignty of males are systematically neglected — without implying that men are superior to, or more valuable than, women.

### Structure

**Act I — The Question** (0:00–4:00) · S01–S05
Cold open over a sleeping city; the ordinary men who hold it up; the film states plainly what it is not; *integrity* traced to *integer*; the ten integrities built as a geodesic that compensates when one member dims.

**Act II — The Organism and Its Conditions** (4:00–7:50) · S06–S10
Ethical capacity has a physical address; the unglamorous things, with the limit stated explicitly; what the data shows, and what it doesn't; the body as downstream of its environment; every institution's unwritten curriculum.

**Act III — The Ethical Ecology** (7:50–11:05) · S11–S14
The machinery that makes trust rational; **ethical burden transfer** rendered as structural load redistribution — the film's centrepiece; trust as infrastructure across 12,000 years in one unbroken walking shot; responsibility held in both hands.

**Act IV — Worth, Identity, and What Remains** (11:05–15:00) · S15–S20
Dignity mistaken for accounting; the gate with no walls; fifty years in one locked-off frame; the delta that is a lung; the opening shot reversed; one warm line, named once, then out.
