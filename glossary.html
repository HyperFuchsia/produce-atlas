Terminology governanceHow to read this glossary

Some Whole Man terms are established in medicine, psychology, ethics, law, public health, disability studies, or social science. Others adapt established concepts, and some are framework terms developed to name recurring mechanisms. A new term is legitimate only when it identifies an observable pattern, is defined precisely, distinguishes itself from related concepts, does not claim clinical or legal status it does not possess, and remains open to correction.

Glossary rule

Whole Man names patterns so they can be examined. It does not declare a pattern proven merely because a term has been assigned to it.

Status of coined terms

Coined terms are original synthesis, not standardized scientific or legal constructs. In particular, “failure coding,” “masculinity capture,” “pathologizing mislocation,” “relational self-abandonment,” and “human-value extraction” are project terms — not formal diagnoses and not universally standardized legal categories. See the full research caveats and the claim-grading standard.

Each entry below carries the status the framework’s own terminology governance assigns it:

Established
Established in medicine, psychology, ethics, law, public health, disability studies, or social science (including established concepts the framework uses in a framework-specific way, which are marked as such).
Framework term / protocol / standard / distinction / ethical direction
Coined or defined by the project to name a recurring mechanism, method, or boundary. Original synthesis — open to revision, narrowing, or retirement if the evidence demands it.
Core concept
One of the framework’s foundational Part II definitions. The one-line version appears here; the full treatment, including the non-negotiable distinctions around it, lives on Dignity & sovereignty.
Master lexicon (status not assigned)
Listed in the framework’s master lexicon without an assigned status classification.

Where a term’s home page treats it at length, the entry links there. The glossary is a map, not the territory.

These are the current canonical one-line meanings of the framework’s decisive terms, from the July 22, 2026 dossier. Where any older or broader formulation elsewhere appears to conflict with these, these govern.

AccountabilityEvidence-based, proportionate truth-finding, protection, consequence, repair where possible, and prevention. See Dignity & sovereignty.

MasculinityPlural individual and cultural relationships to being or being perceived as male; not a qualification test for dignity. See Many male lives.

Non-negotiable distinctions

Do not confuse a person’s existence with his conduct. Do not confuse dignity with immunity. Do not confuse sovereignty with unilateral authority. Do not confuse calmness with truth.
