HOW TO PLAY

▲
VAULT / JUMP

Tap the top half of the screen or swipe up, or press Space / ↑. Hold longer to jump higher. Hit a barrier mid-air and Marcus hand-plants into an automatic vault.

▼
DIVE / SLIDE

Tap the bottom half or swipe down, or press ↓ / S. On the ground you slide under beams. In the air you dive — fast, low, and hard enough to smash through glass panels.

↔
SWITCH LANES

Swipe left or right, or press ← → / A D. Some walls are too tall to jump and too low to slide under — the only way past is the lane beside them. Shards mark the safe line.

◆
FLOW

Late vaults, close-call dives and shard chains build the Flow meter. Fill it to enter FLOW STATE: double score and a shockwave that shrugs off one impact.

✦
SHARDS

Spend them in GEAR on permanent upgrades and looks. Everything you unlock is yours forever — no timers, no energy.
