What is actually running

Reel one holds a 7-dimensional complex state vector; reels two and three share a single 49-dimensional one. Between observations it evolves under a fixed unitary — two-level rotations on a ring plus per-site phases — so the odds are never the same twice. The lever applies the Born rule: the chance of an outcome is |ψᵢ|², weighted by a per-species detector efficiency η, and the reel then collapses onto whatever you saw.

The Zeno trap

Collapse leaves a reel sitting in a single basis state. Observe again immediately and it has barely evolved, so it repeats — the quantum Zeno effect (Misra & Sudarshan, 1977). Burst mode sits deep in that regime and the reels visibly stick. It feels like a cheat. It is not: see the next card.

Why you cannot game the index

Your match count minus its running expectation is a martingale. Each spin's probability is computed from the state as it stood just before that spin, so the variances still add — Σp(1−p) — even though Zeno makes consecutive spins strongly correlated. Freezing the reels raises your matches and your expectation by exactly the same amount. Better still, a locked reel has p near 0 or 1, so p(1−p) ≈ 0 and it adds almost no evidence — which is why the index is gated on Σp(1−p) rather than on how many times you pulled the lever.

Instruments on the panel

l₁ coherence (Baumgratz, Cramer & Plenio, 2014) is zero the instant you measure and climbs as the state spreads back out. Entanglement is the real thing: reels two and three share one 49-dimensional state, coupled by a phase that will not separate, and the meter is 1−Tr ρ² of one reel's reduced state. Leggett–Garg K tests your hit sequence against macrorealism, bound at K ≤ 1 — and no sequence you can produce will break it.

The background

Behind the glass turns a tesseract: a 4-cube of 16 vertices and 32 edges, rotated in the xw and zw planes and projected 4→3→2. Its rate is tied to the live coherence, so when the machine decoheres the fourth dimension slows down with it. The falling glyphs are the alphabet the state is written in.

Honest disclosure

This simulates the formalism; it is not quantum hardware. The randomness is your browser's PRNG and the amplitudes are floating point. Everything described above is genuinely computed and nothing is faked for effect — and there is no stake, credit, wager or payout anywhere in it.
