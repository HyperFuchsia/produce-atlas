Specimen 001  ·  the term as received

artificial intelligence

Two words. Both wrong.

Plate I

A naming error

Not a complaint about hype. A complaint about a noun.

Hanover, New Hampshire  ·  August 1955

Four men need a name for a summer workshop.

  • 01John McCarthy  Dartmouth
  • 02Marvin Minsky  Harvard
  • 03Nathaniel Rochester  IBM
  • 04Claude Shannon  Bell Labs

Rejected on the page

  • Acybernetics
  • Bautomata studies
  • Ccomplex information processing
  • Dthinking machines
  • ↵artificial intelligence

“every aspect of learning or any other feature of intelligence can in principle be so precisely described that a machine can be made to simulate it”

Dartmouth Summer Research Project proposal  ·  1955

Not a method. A bet.

He wasn’t naming a science.

He was planting a flag.

Word one

Artificial

  • —artificial flavour
  • —artificial turf
  • —artificial sweetener

Every usage means the same thing:
a stand-in for something it is not.

Nothing about it is imitation.

01
The arithmetic is real.
02
The electricity is real.
03
The output is real.

“Artificial” smuggles in a verdict:
that the genuine article is somewhere else.

Word two

Intelligence

A century of argument in psychology. No agreed definition. We used it as a product category.

Try it with any other contested word.

A branch of engineering called
artificial happiness

You would be asked to define your terms
immediately, and forever.

Plate II  ·  the counterfactual

So let’s do it to biology instead.

1828  ·  Friedrich Wöhler

NH4OCN→(NH2)2CO

An organic compound, made from dead mineral salt. Until that morning it had only ever come out of a living body.

“I can no longer, so to speak, hold my chemical water and must tell you that I can make urea without needing a kidney, whether of man or dog.”

Wöhler to Berzelius  ·  1828

Vitalism took a mortal wound that day.
A bigger shock than anything a chatbot has managed.

Imagine they had named it after the destination

Artificial
Biology

Hereafter: AB

The press this name would have produced

  • Science deskAB lab achieves breakthrough in self-replication
  • OpinionIs AB conscious? Researchers remain divided
  • WorldNations convene first global AB safety summit
  • MarketsAB firm raises $4bn at $70bn valuation

Biology is a study. You cannot synthesise a study.

  • Journal of Artificial Botany
  • Institute for Artificial Geology
  • Dept. of Artificial Astronomy
  • Review of Artificial Meteorology

These phrases are noise. So is ours.

The goalposts, in AB world

  • 1978yeast that makes insulin just fermentation
  • 1983PCR just enzymes
  • 2020mRNA vaccines just chemistry

Nothing is ever AB once it works.

The goalposts, in our world

  • 1997chess just tree search
  • 2000sreading handwriting just pattern matching
  • Dailyspam, routing, autocomplete just software

Nothing is ever AI once it works.

“AI is whatever hasn’t been done yet.”

Attributed to Larry Tesler

A name that evaporates on contact with success.

No other field disowns its own results.

Chemistry keeps the periodic table.
Medicine keeps antibiotics.
This is the only discipline that fires its graduates.

AB would have spent sixty years arguing about whether the flask was truly alive.

Instead of making insulin.

We are running that experiment right now. On ourselves.

Plate III  ·  the fields that got it right

Aviation never called itself artificial flight.

It named the mechanism instead:
lift  ·  thrust  ·  aerofoil  ·  angle of attack

“The question of whether machines can think is about as relevant as the question of whether submarines can swim.”

Edsger W. Dijkstra  ·  1984

Nobody built an artificial fish.
They built a submarine, and “swim” stopped mattering.

And when biology really did build life-like systems

Synthetic
biology

Named for the method, not the ambition.
Nobody had to hold a summit about it.

Good names describe a mechanism.

Bad names describe an ambition.

We picked the ambition.

Plate IV  ·  the mechanism, stated plainly

A function, fitted to data, by walking downhill across a surface of its own error.

That is the whole mechanism. Nothing has been left out.

None of them promise anything they cannot deliver.

The name is not cosmetic.

01
It sets the funding.

We are regulating a metaphor — and the metaphor was
chosen by four men who needed a title for a workshop.

We did not build artificial intelligence.

We built something genuinely new — and gave it someone else’s name.
