Everything in teal exists and runs today. Everything in red is a gate that no amount of further building opens — each one needs a person: an attorney, a licensed engineer, an underwriter. That is the honest shape of the thing. The method is finished well ahead of the business, which is normal, and worth knowing rather than discovering at the first invoice.

§2

Stage by stage

what runs · what it makes · what stops it
01
Publish the standard
doctrine → standard UAS-1:v0.1 registry schema

You cannot buy authority, so you publish into it. Free, versioned, citable, mapped clause-by-clause to NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, IEC 61508 and the machinery standards — so it reads as an extension of the existing canon rather than a new religion.

02
Create demand
checklist · articles a lead who already believes you

The self-assessment does the arguing. A plant manager discovers on their own that machinery which can kill someone is held by a procedure — and the flagship article tells them the profession settled this decades ago, in their own vocabulary.

03
Scope the engagement
engagement-scope.md GATE — contract terms, E&O

Deliverables, exclusions, client obligations, findings handling. Level 3 is declared unattainable in the first meeting rather than the last. Payment terms, liability, indemnity and IP are attorney work — the skeleton stops exactly where template work stops being honest.

04
Enumerate on site
field-guide.md 1.5–2.5 days registry JSON

The four attribution questions, chains traced past the first hop, and the six questions that break a false Level 3 claim. This is the only stage that cannot be automated and the only one where the value is actually created — everything downstream is arithmetic on what gets written down here.

05
Run the pipeline
pipeline engagement report finding letters certificate decision manifest

One command, complete package. Generated rather than written so nothing is softened, dropped, or forgotten between the walk and the documents — and so no finding can be quietly removed to keep a client comfortable.

06
Deliver findings
one letter per finding client only

Private, always. No regulator, no insurer, no third party. Enforcement is the absence of a certificate — which is how boiler, elevator and electrical listing have all worked for a century, and it means the assessor never acts against the client who hired them.

07
Remediate and re-score
client or third party not the assessor

In the worked example, two of three criticals were fixed by moving an existing control — the rule was already correct, it just lived inside the system it governed. That is usually true, and saying it early keeps the conversation about engineering rather than blame.

08
Certify
Level 2 — issuable now GATE — licensed PE for Level 3

The scorer takes a deployment to Level 2 and refuses to go further, reporting the PE signature as a blocker it cannot clear. A tool that could award its own top grade would be worth nothing.

09
Open the channel
GATE — carrier relationship inbound, permanently

One carrier crediting certification ends the selling. Clients arrive because a broker sent them, authority is settled by the requirement rather than by reputation, and certification becomes cheaper than the premium differential — free, in the buyer's arithmetic.

Why generated, not written

Findings come out of the registry mechanically. Nobody drafts them, so nobody can soften one, forget one, or quietly drop the awkward one to keep a client comfortable at the close-out meeting. The pressure to do exactly that is the reason inspection regimes fail, and it is strongest precisely when the finding matters most.

§5

The four gates

none of these open by building more
Gate 1 · before any invoice Entity, contract, E&O

Legal entity and bank account. Payment, liability, indemnity and IP terms drafted by an attorney. Errors-and-omissions cover quoted against the corrected claims language — which is also why every absolute claim had to go: they are what make cover unaffordable or void.

Gate 2 · before Level 3 A licensed engineer

A certification body's product is the credibility of a signature, and it has to belong to someone personally exposed. Also the person who validates every [PE-REVIEW] threshold — which is the honest way to open the conversation.

Gate 3 · the flywheel One carrier

Everything in stages 1–8 is preparation for one conversation with an underwriter. Hartford Steam Boiler, 1866: inspect and carry the risk. Skin in the game is what made the inspection credible then and it is what would make it credible now.

Gate 0 · before committing a year Verify the market is empty

D-0 item 7 rests on reasoning, not research, and it is load-bearing. Check whether any equipment-breakdown carrier already asks about automated systems on its inspection forms — that single check also tells you whether Gate 3 is open.

The thing to be clear about

More building does not open any of these. The method is further along than the business, and the remaining work is three conversations — an attorney, an engineer, an underwriter — plus one piece of research. Continuing to build instead would be the comfortable move and the wrong one.

The worked examples are illustrative and clearly labelled as such. They demonstrate the method; they are not a track record, and presenting them as one would be the first dishonest thing in the whole system.
No conformance level under this standard eliminates risk. Clause 10.5.2 makes that binding on the certifying body, not advisory.
