### Scope

This certificate applies to the actuators enumerated in registry version stated
above, and to no others. It attests that those actuators satisfy the
requirements of the stated conformance level as at the date of issue.

### This certificate is void on any of the following

Per Clause 10.4.3 — the holder must notify the certifying body within 5 working
days of any of these:

- Addition of an actuator not present in the assessed registry
- Increase in any rated capacity
- Reduction in the control level of any Tier 1 or Tier 2 actuator
- Lapse of proof testing beyond the interval at Clause 7.8.2

### What this certificate does not attest

**This certificate does not state, and must not be represented as stating, that
catastrophic failure has been eliminated, that mitigation is complete, or that
residual risk is zero** (Clause 10.5.2).

It makes no assurance regarding model quality, accuracy, or fairness within
rated capacity, nor regarding any actuator not enumerated.
